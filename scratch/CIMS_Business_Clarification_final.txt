BR-18 / BR-09
Insurance reminder periods
“Generate configurable reminder schedules” / “Reminder intervals shall be configurable without system customization.”
What are the reminder intervals before policy expiry? Do they vary by business segment or collateral type?
Reminder intervals before policy expiry:
The standard reminder schedule shall be as follows:
30 working days before expiry: First reminder.
25 working days before expiry: Second reminder.
20 working days before expiry: Third reminder.
Every other working day thereafter: Continue sending reminders until the policy is renewed or otherwise resolved.
The reminder intervals shall be standardized across all business segments and collateral types, unless a different schedule is explicitly configured through the system.
To avoid multiple notifications on the same day, the system shall send a maximum of Two reminder notification per day. One at the beginning of the day and the other at mid-day. 
Where multiple policies/items are due for reminder on the same day, they shall be consolidated into a single notification containing a summary list, categorized by the applicable reminder stage.
The customer shall receive notifications via SMS and/or email every five (5) days, starting 30 days before the policy expiry date, and continuing until the policy is renewed or otherwise resolved.
The system shall ensure that no more than one customer notification is sent per day. Notifications shall be automatically discontinued once the policy has been renewed or otherwise resolved.
BR-18 / BR-07 / BR-09
Escalation timelines
“Escalate overdue renewals with define escalation Metrix” 
What is the actual escalation matrix — who escalates to whom, and after how many days overdue?
Escalation Sequence:
The system shall implement the following escalation sequence for policies that have not been renewed:
First Escalation – 10 Days Before Expiry
If the policy has not been renewed by 10 calendar days before the expiry date, the system shall escalate the matter to the Relationship Manager (RM), with the owning Relationship Officer (RO) copied on the notification.
Second Escalation – 5 Days Later
If the policy remains unresolved for a further 5 days, the system shall escalate the matter to the Branch Manager or Corporate Center Manager, as applicable.
Further Escalation – Every 15 days
the system shall escalate more than ten days’ overdue cases to the respective District Business Manager or Area Manager copied the respective manager. With a summary per area office and branch including how long they are overdue.
Further escalations shall occur every 15 days thereafter, with all policies due for escalation consolidated into a single summary report.
The system shall ensure that escalation notifications are sent no more than once per day and that multiple policies reaching the same escalation stage on the same day are consolidated into a single notification or summary report.

BR-18 / BR-15
User roles — permission matrix
Roles are named (BR-15) but “The system shall control: Screen access, Menu access, Data visibility, Transaction authorization, Approval authority, Report access, Document access” with no matrix
What is the permission matrix — which role gets which screen, data, approval, and report access?
Permission Matrix Overview:
- Relationship Officer (RO): Maker access. Can register policies, upload documents, generate reminders. No approval rights.
- Relationship Manager (RM)/ Branch Manager: Checker access. Verifies RO entries, approves, views segment portfolio, authorizes policy cancellations.
- District/Operations Manager/ Area Manager: Views district portfolio, approves district-level exceptions, monitors renewals.
- Risk/Compliance/Auditor: Read-only access to all dashboards, compliance reports and audit trails.
- System Admin: Full configuration access, user management, no transactional approval rights.

BR-06
Mandatory insurance by collateral type
“Insurance is mandatory based on collateral type”
For which collateral types is insurance mandatory?
Insurance is mandatory for almost all tangible collateral types:
1. Buildings/Real Estate: Fire and Lightning insurance is mandatory.
2. Vehicles: Comprehensive Motor Insurance is mandatory.
3. Construction Machinery: All-risk comprehensive insurance is mandatory.
4. Merchandise/Inventory: Fire, lightning, burglary, and spontaneous combustion insurance is mandatory.
5. Movable Properties: Comprehensive insurance cover is mandatory before EMCR registration.
For financial assets/cash, insurance is generally not required as the asset itself is the cover
BR-06
Policy validity vs. lending requirements
“Policy validity period meets lending requirements”
What is the minimum policy validity period relative to the loan tenor?
As per the Credit Processing Procedure Manual (Section 12.4.4):
1. General Rule: The insurance policy coverage shall not be less than one (1) year.
2. Short-term Loans (Merchandise/Shipments): The policy validity period shall not be less than the loan tenor, which depends on the nature of the items.3. Policy validity period must be >= 1 year for term loans, or equal to loan tenor for short-term loans (merchandise/shipments).

BR-08
Supporting evidence (document category)
“Supporting evidence” listed as a document type with no further definition
What does “supporting evidence” cover in this context?
Supporting evidence covers documents that prove the authenticity or status of an insurance policy or collateral, including:
1. Premium Payment Receipts: Proof that the insurance premium has been paid in full.
2. Insurance Claim Documents: Documents related to any claims made on the collateral (e.g., accident reports, fire reports).
3. Correspondence with Insurance Companies: Emails or letters regarding policy renewals.
BR-10
Expired-policy notification interval
“Expired policies with predefined interval” — the interval itself is never given
What is the notification interval/frequency for expired policies?
For policies that have already expired:
1. Internal Staff (RM/RO/Branch Manager): The system shall send daily notifications for the first 5 days after expiry, and then weekly summaries until resolved.
2. Customers: The system shall send notifications every 5 days (as per the first item in the log) via SMS and/or email until the policy is renewed or the collateral is substituted.
3. Escalations: District/Area Managers shall receive consolidated escalation reports every 15 days for policies overdue by more than 10 days.
