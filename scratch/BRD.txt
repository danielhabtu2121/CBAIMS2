Introduction
The Collateral Insurance Management System (CIMS) is a strategic initiative designed to automate and streamline the end-to-end management of collateral insurance across the Bank. The solution aims to replace manual and fragmented processes with a centralized, integrated, and workflow-driven platform that manages the complete insurance lifecycle—from policy registration and monitoring to renewal, expiry management, and policy closure.
CIMS will provide a single source of truth for managing collateral insurance associated with all lending portfolios, including Corporate Banking, Retail Banking, MSME Banking, and Interest-Free Banking (IFB). The system will support both borrower-owned and third-party-owned collateral, including cross-collateralized facilities where a single collateral secures multiple loan accounts.
By integrating with the Bank's Core Banking System (CBS), Document Management System (DMS), SMS Gateway, Email Server, and Business Intelligence (BI) platforms, the solution will facilitate seamless information exchange, automate business processes, and provide real-time visibility into collateral insurance compliance across the Bank.
The implementation of CIMS is intended to strengthen risk management and regulatory compliance by ensuring that pledged collateral remains adequately insured throughout the loan lifecycle. Through configurable workflows, automated notifications, maker-checker approvals, comprehensive audit trails, and enterprise-wide dashboards, the system will improve operational efficiency, enhance accountability, and support proactive decision-making.
Specifically, the implementation of CIMS is expected to:
Achieve end-to-end automation of the collateral insurance management process.
Ensure continuous insurance coverage of pledged collateral through proactive monitoring and timely policy renewals.
Reduce operational and credit risks associated with expired, missing, or inadequate insurance coverage.
Strengthen compliance with the Bank's internal credit policies, governance framework, and applicable regulatory requirements.
Improve operational efficiency by replacing manual tracking and follow-up activities with automated workflows, notifications, and exception management.
Enhance customer experience through timely communication, simplified renewal processes, and improved service delivery.
Increase transparency and accountability through comprehensive dashboards, management reports, and complete audit trails.
Support informed decision-making by providing real-time management information, compliance monitoring, and actionable exception reporting.
Establish a secure, integrated, scalable, and enterprise-wide platform that aligns with the Bank's digital transformation strategy, enterprise architecture, and governance standards.
This Business Requirements Document (BRD) defines the business needs, functional capabilities, business rules, integration requirements, and non-functional requirements necessary for the successful design, development, implementation, and deployment of the Collateral Insurance Management System.
Functional Requirements Summary
The Collateral Insurance Management System (CIMS) will provide end-to-end automation of the collateral insurance lifecycle, from policy registration to renewal and closure. The solution will capture and manage customer, loan, collateral, and insurance policy information while maintaining all related documents in a centralized repository. Detail specification will be presented in the BRD
The system will automate insurance expiry monitoring through configurable reminders and escalation workflows, sending notifications via SMS, email, and in-system alerts to Relationship Managers, customers, and insurance companies. It will also support maker-checker approval workflows, policy renewals, and maintain a complete audit trail of all transactions.
The solution will enforce business rules related to insurance eligibility, coverage adequacy, policy renewal, approval hierarchies, and compliance requirements. Role-based access will ensure users perform activities according to their responsibilities, including Relationship Managers, Relationship Officers, Branch Managers, and System Administrators.
The system will integrate with the Core Banking System (CBS), SMS Gateway, Email Server, Document Management System, and Reporting/Business Intelligence platforms to enable seamless data exchange and process automation.
Comprehensive dashboards and reports will provide real-time visibility into insured collateral, policy expirations, renewal status, compliance, and operational performance. The solution will also identify and manage exceptions Detail specification will be presented in the BRD 
Finally, the system will incorporate robust security controls, including user authentication, role-based access control, audit logging, data protection, and information confidentiality, ensuring compliance with the Bank's governance, risk management, and regulatory requirements.
Business Requirement Overview
The Collateral Insurance Management System (CIMS) shall automate and digitize the end-to-end management of collateral insurance throughout the lending lifecycle. The solution shall ensure that all collateral pledged against credit facilities remains adequately insured at all times while improving operational efficiency, strengthening regulatory compliance, minimizing operational risk, and providing management with real-time visibility into insurance status across the Bank.
The system shall eliminate manual tracking of insurance policies, reduce dependency on spreadsheets and manual follow-up, improve customer service through timely notifications, and ensure continuous insurance coverage of pledged collateral.
Detail Business Requirements (BR)
BR-01 Bank-wide and Business Segment Management
The Collateral Insurance Management System (CIMS) shall support centralized, bank-wide management of collateral insurance while accommodating the operational responsibilities of the Bank's different lending business segments.
The solution shall support the following business segments:
Corporate Banking 
Retail Banking 
MSME Banking 
Interest-Free Banking (IFB) 
Any additional lending segment configured by the Bank 
The system shall:
Maintain ownership of each collateral insurance record by the responsible business segment. 
Assign Relationship Managers, Relationship Officers, Credit Officers, and Branch users according to the respective business segment. 
Support segment-specific insurance monitoring, follow-up, renewal, and reporting. 
Allow Head Office users to monitor collateral insurance across all business segments. 
Enable segment heads and management to monitor insurance compliance within their respective portfolios. 
Support configurable business rules that may vary by business segment, including approval hierarchy, reminder schedules, and insurance requirements. 
Generate both Bank-wide and segment-specific dashboards and management reports. 
Maintain a consolidated view of collateral insurance exposure across the Bank while preserving segment accountability. 
Allow transfer of portfolio ownership between business segments with complete audit history. 
BR-02 Customer, Loan and Collateral Information Management
The system shall provide centralized management of all information required for collateral insurance administration.
The solution shall:
Capture customer information from the Core Banking System (CBS) or manually captured 
Capture loan and credit facility details from CBS 
Retrieve collateral information linked to each loan 
Maintain collateral ownership information 
Maintain collateral valuation details 
Maintain collateral classification 
Support multiple collateral records under a single loan 
Support multiple loans secured by one collateral where applicable 
Maintain relationship hierarchy between customer, facility, collateral and insurance policy 
Synchronize master information with CBS automatically 
BR-03 Loan and Collateral Relationship Management
The system shall maintain a complete relationship between collateral assets and the credit facilities secured by them.
The solution shall support:
One collateral securing one loan account. 
One collateral securing multiple loan accounts (Cross-Collateralization). 
Multiple collateral assets securing a single loan account. 
Multiple collateral assets securing multiple loan accounts. 
For every collateral, the system shall maintain:
Linked loan account(s) 
Loan reference number 
Customer information 
Facility type 
Outstanding loan balance 
Sanction amount 
Collateral allocation amount 
Collateral utilization percentage 
Business segment ownership 
Branch ownership 
Relationship Manager 
For cross-collateralized facilities, the solution shall:
Display all linked loan accounts from a single collateral record. 
Display all collateral linked to a selected loan account. 
Calculate the total secured exposure against the collateral. 
Monitor insurance adequacy against the aggregate exposure of all linked facilities. 
Prevent duplicate collateral registration. 
Support release of collateral only after validating all linked facilities. 
Automatically update all linked loan relationships whenever collateral information changes. 
Maintain historical linkage records for audit purposes. 
BR-04 Collateral Ownership and Ownership Document Management
The Collateral Insurance Management System (CIMS) shall maintain comprehensive ownership information for every collateral asset pledged to secure the Bank's lending facilities, regardless of whether the collateral is owned by the borrower or by a third party.
The solution shall support both:
Borrower-owned collateral – where the borrower is the legal owner of the pledged collateral. 
Third-party collateral – where the collateral is legally owned by an individual or organization other than the borrower but has been pledged as security for the borrower's credit facility. 
The system shall capture and maintain:
Ownership Information
Ownership type (Borrower-owned or Third-party-owned) 
Legal owner name 
Owner type (Individual, Business Organization, Government Entity, or Other) 
National ID, Passport, or Business Registration Number 
Tax Identification Number (where applicable) 
Contact details of the owner 
Ownership percentage (for jointly owned collateral) 
Ownership verification status 
Relationship between the borrower and the collateral owner (e.g., Shareholder, Guarantor, Parent Company, Director, Spouse, Family Member, Business Partner, or Other) 
Date of ownership verification 
Ownership remarks 
Change of ownership, if there is any
Third-Party Collateral Management
Where collateral is owned by a third party, the system shall:
Capture the third-party owner's details. 
Link the collateral owner to the borrower and the associated loan account(s). 
Record the legal relationship between the borrower and the collateral owner. 
Capture the third-party consent and pledge information. 
Maintain the status of all required legal documentation supporting the pledge. 
Allow one third-party collateral to secure one or multiple loan accounts, including cross-collateralized facilities. 
Maintain a complete audit trail of any changes to ownership information. 
Ownership Document Management
The system shall support uploading, storing, and managing scanned copies of ownership and legal documents, including but not limited to:
Title Deed 
Property Ownership Certificate 
Vehicle Registration Certificate 
Machinery Ownership Certificate 
Share Certificate 
Warehouse Receipt 
Business Registration Certificate 
Lease Agreement (where applicable) 
Third-Party Collateral Agreement
Letter of Consent from the Third-Party Owner 
Mortgage Registration Documents 
Court Registration Documents (where applicable) 
Any additional supporting legal documents required by the Bank 
The solution shall provide:
Multiple document attachments for each collateral. 
High-resolution scanned document storage. 
Version control and document history. 
Document preview and retrieval. 
Secure access based on user roles. 
Document expiry monitoring (where applicable). 
The system shall prevent collateral registration or insurance policy activation where mandatory ownership or legal documents are missing unless an authorized exception has been approved in accordance with the Bank's approval hierarchy.

The system shall support complete lifecycle management of collateral insurance policies.
The solution shall enable users to:
Assign unique ID for each customer
Register new insurance policies 
Capture insurer information 
Capture policy details 
Record policy number 
Record coverage type 
Record insured amount 
Record premium information 
Record policy effective date 
Record policy expiry date 
Maintain policy status 
Register endorsements 
Capture policy amendments 
Record policy cancellation 
Record policy replacement 
Process policy renewals 
Close expired or cancelled policies 
Reopening of closed policies
Maintain complete historical records 
BR-06 Insurance Eligibility and Compliance Validation
The system shall automatically validate insurance compliance before accepting policy registration or renewal.
The solution shall verify:
Insurance is mandatory based on collateral type 
Insurance company is approved by the Bank 
Coverage amount satisfies Bank policy 
Policy validity period meets lending requirements 
Collateral valuation supports insured amount 
Duplicate policies are prevented 
Multiple Policies of a single customer are linked to single unique customer ID 
Expired policies cannot be accepted 
Mandatory documents are available 
Customer and collateral information are complete 
Regulatory requirements are satisfied 
Validation failures shall prevent workflow progression unless overridden by authorized personnel.

BR-09 Insurance Renewal and Expiry Management
The system shall proactively monitor policy validity and automate renewal management.
The solution shall:
Continuously monitor insurance expiry dates 
Generate configurable reminder schedules 
Notify Relationship Managers 
Notify Relationship Officers 
Notify customers 
Notify insurance companies, where possible
Escalate overdue renewals with define escalation Metrix 
Monitor renewal completion 
Support policy replacement 
Track renewal turnaround time 
Generate overdue renewal reports 
Automatically update policy status after renewal 
Reminder intervals shall be configurable without system customization.

Email/Outlook

System-generated alerts

BR-11 Bank wide Collateral Coverage Monitoring
The system shall continuously monitor insurance adequacy for all collateral pledged across the Bank.
The solution shall:
Calculate insurance coverage against the total secured exposure where collateral secures multiple facilities. 
Identify underinsured collateral. 
Identify uninsured collateral. 
Identify duplicate insurance records. 
Monitor expired ownership documents. 
Monitor missing ownership documentation. 
Highlight collateral linked to multiple business segments. 
Produce Bank-wide collateral insurance exposure reports. 
Produce segment-wise collateral coverage reports. 
Produce branch-wise compliance reports. 
Produce Relationship Manager-wise insurance compliance reports. 
Management shall be able to drill down from Bank level to:
Business Segment 
District 
Branch 
Relationship Manager 
Customer Segment
Loan Account 
Individual Collateral
