1. Collateral registration and data source
CBAIMS will be the system where collateral is registered and managed. Collateral records will be created in CBAIMS through a maker-checker workflow. This covers ownership details, valuation details, classification, ownership documents and the link to the loan(s) the collateral secures (BR-02, BR-03, BR-04).
The responsibilities are divided as follows:
CBS (FLEXCUBE 12.5) remains the master for: customer (CIF), loan and facility details, sanction amounts, outstanding balances, loan closure and facility changes. CBAIMS retrieves these through integration.
CBAIMS is the master for: the collateral record, ownership and third-party pledge information, valuation details, collateral-to-loan allocation, insurance policies, documents (stored in Alfresco DMS), workflows, alerts and exceptions.
Existing collateral: collateral already registered in FLEXCUBE or other systems will be loaded into CBAIMS once, then reconciled and validated. Each record will carry a unique Collateral ID, and CBAIMS will prevent duplicate registration (BR-03).
CBAIMS prevails for collateral data and CBS prevails for loan and customer data.
Customer data: pulled from CBS by CIF. Manual capture is a fallback only for parties without a CIF, such as third-party owners.
2. Determination of adequate insurance coverage
The thresholds must come from the Bank’s Credit Policy, so CBAIMS should hold them as configurable coverage rules (BR-18) by collateral type and segment, not hard-code them. The recommended logic is:
Primary test: insured amount against the Bank-assessed value of the insurable portion of the collateral. For a building, this is the structure without the land. Required coverage = Bank-assessed insurable value × required % (default 100%). [confirm %]
Secondary test (BR-03/BR-11): insured amount against the aggregate outstanding exposure of all facilities secured by the collateral. This means cross-collateralized facilities are monitored on total exposure.
Estimated value is for reference only and is not used in the adequacy test.
Statuses: Adequate, Underinsured (below the required %), Uninsured and Over insured (above a configurable tolerance, e.g. [X]%). Over insurance is an informational flag and does not block anything.
Exceptions: yes. If the Bank accepts underinsured or over insured collateral, the user must record a reason and approved exception amount, a validity period and review date, and obtain supervisor approval through the BR-07/BR-12 workflow. The exception is reported until it expires or is resolved.

3.Early release of collateral. Because CBAIMS manages the collateral, it will also record and route the release request. Release is approved through the CBAIMS maker-checker workflow. Once approved, the linkage to the loan shall be removed from the CBS as well (to be done manually). The proposed conditions are below.
Release is approved by the authorized credit authority.
The remaining collateral still meets the required coverage and margin for all linked facilities.
For cross-collateral, all linked facilities have been validated (BR-03). Release is blocked if any fails.
There is no unresolved insurance exception or pending insurance claim.
Release documents are filed in DMS.
CBAIMS will show a pre-release impact check (coverage after release) and warn if the check fails. After release, it closes monitoring.
4. First-degree and second-degree collateral
CBAIMS will maintain the first-degree/second-degree relationship, because the insurance and exposure checks depend on it. For a second-degree record, CBAIMS will hold:
Rank (1st, 2nd) and a flag for whether the first-degree charge is internal (held by the Bank) or external (held by another institution)
First-degree holder’s name
First-degree facility reference and outstanding balance, with an “as of” date
Registered mortgage amount, registration number and date
First-degree insurance status: policy number, insurer, expiry, and whether the Bank is noted as second mortgagee
Data source:
Internal first-degree: the first-degree collateral is already a CBAIMS record, so the second-degree record links to it directly. Only the first-degree loan balance is retrieved from CBS.
External first-degree: captured manually in CBAIMS as a separate registration step, supported by the other institution’s confirmation letter and a registration extract in DMS. Balances must be refreshed periodically.
Validation rules:
A second-degree record cannot be created without a first-degree record and supporting evidence.
Rank must be unique per collateral.
Combined exposure (first plus second) must not exceed the collateral’s Bank-assessed value or a warning is raised.
Insured amount is tested against the combined exposure.
If an internal first-degree charge is released or closed, CBAIMS flags the second-degree charge for promotion and review.
